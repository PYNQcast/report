\section{Results \& Discussion}

Preliminary benchmarks (Table~\ref{tab:results}) confirm the architecture meets its real-time targets under two-player load.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{System performance summary (2-player session).}
\label{tab:results}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\columnwidth}{>{\raggedright}X r r}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Measured} & \textbf{Target} \\
\midrule
Render frame rate & 62\,fps & $\geq$60\,fps \\
\AltRow Server tick jitter ($\sigma$) & 1.2\,ms & $<$5\,ms \\
RTT (LAN) & 8\,ms & $<$50\,ms \\
\AltRow Redis write latency (p99) & 1.8\,ms & $<$5\,ms \\
DynamoDB flush (p99) & 42\,ms & async \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The monotonic tick scheduler reduced jitter standard deviation from 6.3\,ms to 1.2\,ms (Figure~\ref{fig:jitter}).  Decoupling Redis writes from the game loop via the async sidecar eliminated the 3--8\,ms stalls previously observed during tick processing.
